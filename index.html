Our Authentic Rural Roots

Insaf Milk is a family-owned domestic dairy farm nestled in the heart of Uttara,Dhaka, Bangladesh. We believe that true health begins with nature. That's why our cows are raised in a stress-free rural environment, fed with natural green fodder, and cared for with utmost dedication.

We stand firmly against the commercialization that strips milk of its natural goodness. When you choose Insaf Milk, you are choosing zero artificial additives, no preservatives, and 100% unadulterated purity. It's milk the way nature intended.
